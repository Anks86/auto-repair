Privacy at Babbal Auto Repair

This page explains what the website collects, why it is needed, who can see it, and how long it is kept.

Last updated August 19, 2026

On this page

What we collect How it is used Where it goes Retention Your choices Contact

What we collect

If you send the service-request form, it asks for your name, phone number, preferred reply method, vehicle year, make and model, municipality or postal code, and a short description of the help you need.

Do not send payment card details, government identification, medical information, or other sensitive information through the form.

How the information is used

Babbal uses the submitted details only to review your request, decide whether the mobile task may be suitable, contact you, arrange timing, and prevent misuse of the form.

The website does not use advertising trackers or analytics. It does not sell customer information.

Where the information goes

Babbal and one administrator

The form sends one email to babbalautorepair@gmail.com. Babbal and one authorized administrator can access these requests.

Cloudflare

Cloudflare hosts and protects the website. Cloudflare Turnstile checks browser and device signals to reduce automated spam. Cloudflare states that Turnstile does not access, store, or transmit form entries or other page inputs. Read Cloudflare's Turnstile overview.

Google

Google provides the Gmail mailbox that receives requests. Google handles that email under its own privacy policy.

These providers may process limited technical or email information in Canada and other countries where they operate.

How long requests are kept

The website has no customer database and does not keep a separate copy of a submitted request. Request emails are deleted from the active mailbox after 90 days. Service providers may retain limited security or backup records under their own policies.

How information is protected

The live website will use HTTPS, server-side form validation, spam protection, limited mailbox access, security headers, and secrets that are kept outside the public website files. No internet or email system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices

  • You can call or text instead of using the website form.
  • You can ask what personal information Babbal has about you.
  • You can ask for a correction or deletion, subject to any information Babbal must keep for a valid legal or operational reason.
  • You can make a privacy complaint and ask for a response.
